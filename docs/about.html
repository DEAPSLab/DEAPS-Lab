About POEMS Lab

Parallel, Operating, Edge, and Mobile Systems Lab — a world-class research laboratory pioneering innovations in distributed systems, edge computing, and real-time technologies.

Our Mission

To conduct world-class research in distributed and real-time systems while fostering the next generation of computer scientists and engineers who will shape the future of technology.

We bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical applications, creating solutions that have real-world impact across industries.
